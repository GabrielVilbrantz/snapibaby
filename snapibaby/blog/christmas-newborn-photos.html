Tips for Taking the Perfect Source Phone Photo

The AI does the heavy lifting, but a good source photo gives it the best material to work with. Here's how to get first christmas baby photos that translate beautifully:

  • Use warm, natural light: Position baby near a window during the day. Morning or golden-hour light is ideal. Avoid overhead fluorescent lighting, which creates harsh shadows on tiny features.
  • Skip the flash entirely: Flash blows out skin tones and produces red-eye. Natural or soft lamp light will always look better and is far gentler on baby's sensitive eyes.
  • Awake or asleep both work: Wide-eyed and curious captures personality; peacefully sleeping creates that dreamy newborn look. Shoot both and let the AI work its magic on each.
  • Keep the background simple: A plain white wall, a neutral blanket, or a clean floor section gives the AI a clean base to build the holiday scene around.
  • Get close: Fill the frame with baby's face and upper body. The more detail the AI can see, the more accurate and lifelike the final portrait will be.
