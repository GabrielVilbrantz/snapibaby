Everything You're Wondering Before You Order 🤔

Do the AI photos actually look like my baby?

Yes, and this is the most important part. We use your baby's real face as the base. Our AI preserves their actual features: eyes, nose, skin tone, and expressions. We only add the professional studio setting, lighting, and outfit around them. The result looks indistinguishable from an $800+ studio session: moms often say even their own family can't tell the difference.

I only have blurry phone photos from the couch. Will it work?

That's exactly what SnapiBaby was made for. Most of our customers use everyday photos taken on the sofa, in the crib, or on the bed (no professional camera, no studio lighting, no experience needed). The simpler the photo, the more magical the transformation looks. If your photo has decent natural light, you're good to go.

How long does it take to receive the portraits?

Between 15 minutes (Premium plan) and 1 hour (Starter plan). Most moms receive their portraits before baby's next feeding with no 7 to 15 day wait like a traditional studio. You order. You wait less than a nap. You open something beautiful.

Absolutely. 7-day, 100% money-back guarantee, no questions asked. If you open your portraits and they don't take your breath away, just email us and we refund every cent. Zero risk on your end: we take all the risk so you don't have to.

The average US newborn studio session costs $800 to $1,200, plus the scheduling stress, the drive, and often a crying baby under studio lights. SnapiBaby starts at $27 and delivers the same studio-quality result in minutes, from your couch. You keep the money. We keep the stress away.
